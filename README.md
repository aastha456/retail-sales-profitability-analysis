\# Retail Sales and Profitability Analysis



\## Project Overview

This project explores a retail sales dataset to analyse sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and discount patterns. The goal is to identify meaningful business insights and provide data-driven recommendations.



\## Project Objectives

\- Understand and explore the retail sales dataset.

\- Clean and prepare data for analysis.

\- Analyse sales and profit across regions and product sub-categories.

\- Examine monthly sales trends.

\- Use Python and SQL to perform data analysis.

\- Visualize key findings and provide business recommendations.

\## Key Findings



\### Regional Performance

\- The West region recorded the highest total sales of 725,457.82.

\- The West region also generated the highest total profit of 108,418.45.

\- The Central region generated higher sales than the South but lower total profit.



\### Product Profitability

\- Tables recorded the highest total loss of -17,725.48.

\- Bookcases and Supplies also recorded negative total profits.

\- Copiers generated the highest average profit among the analysed sub-categories.



\### Discount Analysis

\- Tables and Bookcases had relatively high average discounts and negative average profits.

\- The results suggest that pricing and discount policies for loss-making products should be reviewed.



\### Monthly Sales Trends

\- November recorded the highest aggregated monthly sales of 352,461.07.

\- December and September also recorded relatively high sales.



\## Business Recommendations

\- Maintain successful sales strategies in the West region and explore opportunities to improve performance in other regions.

\- Review pricing, discounts, and product costs for loss-making sub-categories.

\- Evaluate discount policies at the product level and monitor their impact on profitability.

\- Plan inventory and promotional activities around months with higher sales.
